# Agricultural sustainability metrics: terms

| Term | Meaning | Not to confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| LCA | Life cycle assessment: emissions of a product along its production chain, here per crop and plot (GAIA module Emissão) | CFP, a footprint computed by an outside tool |
| RothC | Rothamsted Carbon model: monthly simulation of soil organic carbon (GAIA module Remoção) | Annual soil management factors in LCA |
| CO2e | CO2 equivalent: any greenhouse gas converted by its global warming potential (GWP) | CO2 alone |
| HI | Harvest index: share of above ground dry biomass that is harvested | Yield |
| LUC | Land use change: a past change of use (forest to crop, for example) whose carbon loss is spread over 20 years | Annual soil management |
| DPM, RPM, HUM | RothC carbon pools: decomposable plant material, resistant plant material, humified organic matter | |
| FYM | Farmyard manure: organic amendment with its own pool split in RothC | Crop residue |
| BAT | Biodiversity Assessment Tool: 43 questions in 3 areas (production area, small non productive area, large non productive area), scored low, medium, or high | |
| EIQ | Environmental Impact Quotient (New York State IPM Program): pesticide impact from toxicity to people, fish, birds, bees, beneficial arthropods, and from persistence, leaching, and runoff | |
| STIR | Soil Tillage Intensity Rating (USDA-NRCS): soil disturbance per tillage operation from speed, depth, disturbed area, and tillage type, summed over the season | Soil management practice |
| CFP | Cool Farm Platform: an outside farm carbon footprint tool | LCA |
