## 5. Business rules

- RN-01 A farm (or plot) may hold several Regenerativo fillings, and at most one active filling is the primary one. [task BE-03]
- RN-02 A clone copies every answer of the filling and is never primary. [task BE-04]
- RN-03 Cancelling the primary filling hands the primary role to another active filling. [task BE-04] [RP-1]
- RN-04 The indicators and their weights are global to the platform, the same for every filling. [task BE-16, by design]
