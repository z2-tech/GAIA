# Comparação · business rules v1

Date: 2026-11-03. Based on: vault notes routed by `/sync route docs/vault`, task BE-63 routed by `/sync route docs/tasks`, meeting 2026-09-01, code in `gaia-api/comparison/`.

## 1. What the feature is

The user picks calculations of one module, by name or by filters (project, farm, plot, crop, crop year), and sees them side by side, next to an anonymous benchmark of the platform.

## 2. Why

- The client wants to see which practice, plot, or farm performs better, and where they stand against others.

## 4. Journey

1. The user opens the comparison and picks a module.
2. They pick calculations, or filters that select them.
3. GAIA shows them side by side, with the benchmark when there is enough data.

## 5. Business rules

- RN-01 A comparison takes up to 20 calculations, picked one by one or by filters (project, farm, plot, crop, crop year). [2026-09-01]
- RN-02 One comparison holds one module; modules never mix. [vault]
- RN-03 The user compares only projects they can access; anything else is refused. [2026-09-01, LGPD (Brazil's data protection law)]
- RN-04 The benchmark is anonymous (average, median, quartiles, minimum, maximum, count, no names) and shows only with 5 or more calculations; below that it says it is not available. [2026-09-01, LGPD]
- RN-05 Results are shown per hectare by default, or per kg of product, or in absolute numbers. [task BE-63]
- RN-06 A calculation whose inputs changed after its result shows as out of date in the comparison. [task BE-63] [RP-1]
