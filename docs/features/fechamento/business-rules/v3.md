## 1. What the feature is

During a project the user makes as many calculations as they want, in any plot (talhão) and module: Emissão (emissions, through LCA, life cycle assessment), Remoção (soil carbon removal, through the RothC model), Regenerativo (regenerative practices score) and Biodiversidade (biodiversity). None of them is "official" while the work goes on.

When the client needs a number to sell or report, the project admin or manager makes a closing (fechamento) of one cut (recorte): one crop and one crop year (ano safra), for example milho 2025. On the comparison selection screen they pick the farms, the plots and, in each plot and module, the calculation that is the real one. The platform takes an area weighted mean, plot to farm to project, and shows one number per module for the project. Confirming locks the chosen calculations and keeps a snapshot of that moment.

The closing goes to audit or not. The project goes on: next year another cut is closed, and the earlier one stays locked inside it. Export, PDF, QR code and auditor read from the closing.

## 3. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cálculo** | Any assessment in any module, for one plot (or a whole farm registered as one plot). The platform does not tell tests from real ones until a closing |
| **Recorte** | A cut: one crop and one crop year, for example milho 2025 |
| **Fechamento** | The record that holds, for one cut of one project, the calculation chosen per plot and module, the weighted means and the snapshot. It has its own state |
| **Foto** | The immutable copy kept at closing: inputs, results, factors and aggregated numbers |
| **Fechado** | A closing without audit, locked |
| **Em verificação** | A closing sent to the auditor, locked |
| **Devolvido** | The auditor asked for corrections; the closing's calculations unlock |
| **Verificado** | The auditor approved. It never reopens |
| **Claim** | A company's public communication of a result, for example in a sustainability report [2026-10-02 35:56]. In GAIA it is insetting: the claim of the year, not of a projection [2026-10-02 38:20] |

"Linha de base" and "cenário BAU (business as usual) / de projeto" stay Remoção terms, the two runs of the soil model [R1 Q3 Paulo]. "Oficial" and "Simulação" are no longer screen states.

## 4. Journey

1. Peterson closes a contract with a client and registers the project (no year), the farms and the plots. A homogeneous farm is one plot the size of the farm [2026-10-02 19:56].
2. The team collects data in the field (web or mobile) and fills the calculations: Emissão per crop and period, Remoção with its modelling window, Regenerativo per crop year. They test as much as they want [2026-10-02 29:52].
3. The client finds a buyer who asks for the carbon of milho 2025 [2026-10-02 15:44].
4. The project admin opens the selection screen, picks milho 2025, the farms, the plots and, in each plot and module, the real calculation [2026-10-02 62:20].
5. The platform shows the summary: footprint and emission per hectare, Remoção in the corn period, the Regenerativo score of the year, with the coverage [2026-10-02 63:48].
6. The admin chooses "close without audit" or "close and send to audit" and confirms [2026-10-02 61:24]. The chosen calculations lock and the snapshot is kept.
7. With audit, the auditor reads, comments, and returns or approves. A returned closing unlocks for the fix and is sent again [2026-10-02 31:48, 56:28].
8. The project goes on. Next year another cut is closed; the earlier one stays locked inside the project [2026-10-02 59:20].

## 6. States

States of one closing:

```
            fechar sem auditoria (admin, gerente)
(seleção) ─────────────────────────────────────► fechado (travado)
    │                                              │
    │                                              └─ reabrir com justificativa (admin, gerente) ─► (seleção)
    │  fechar e enviar para auditoria (admin, gerente)
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────► em verificação (travado)
                                                     │                 │
                         pede correção (auditor) ────┘                 │ aprova (auditor)
                                  ▼                                    ▼
                             devolvido (destravado)               verificado (nunca reabre)
                                  │
                                  └─ corrige e reenvia (admin, gerente) ─► em verificação
```

The project itself has no closing state: it stays open while the contract runs, and GAIA ends it when the contract ends (RN-32). Automatic completion at 100% goes away (RN-18).

| State | Who sees | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| fechado | everyone with access to the project | admin and manager reopen |
| em verificação | everyone with access, the auditor | the auditor asks for corrections or approves |
| devolvido | everyone with access, the auditor | admin, manager, technician correct the same calculations; admin and manager send again |
| verificado | everyone with access, the auditor | nobody changes data |
