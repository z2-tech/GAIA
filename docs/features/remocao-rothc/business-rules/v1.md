# Remoção (RothC) · business rules v1

Date: 2026-11-03. Based on: vault note routed by `/sync route docs/vault`, tasks BE-08 and BE-19 to BE-24 routed by `/sync route docs/tasks`, code in `gaia-api/rothc/`.

Rules marked (P-n) are provisional: they follow the proposal until open point n is decided. The others are settled.

## 1. What the feature is

The technician describes, month by month, the crops, soil cover, and organic inputs of a plot under two scenarios, business as usual and project. GAIA runs RothC (the Rothamsted Carbon model) on both and returns the soil carbon the project removes.

## 2. Why

- The client needs the soil carbon removal the project causes, against what would happen without it.

## 3. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| BAU | Business as usual: the scenario without the project |
| Fallow | A month with no crop cycle |

## 4. Journey

1. The technician creates a Remoção calculation for a plot and sets the monthly window.
2. They fill crop cycles, soil cover, and organic inputs for each scenario.
3. GAIA calculates both scenarios and shows the difference.

## 5. Business rules

### 5.1 Scenarios and climate

- RN-01 A calculation has two scenarios, business as usual and project, in the same monthly window, sharing parameters, climate, and methodology versions. [vault, MVP contract]
- RN-02 Climate and radiation come only from the farm coordinates. [vault]

### 5.2 Plant input

- RN-03 Productivity is the harvested mass per hectare in the crop's natural unit; a fresh product crop is converted to dry matter with its dry matter fraction before the residue formula. [RD-4] [vault]
- RN-04 Above ground residue follows the harvest index formula, with 100% kept on the field; roots and rhizodeposition are left out. [RD-4] [vault]
- RN-05 An annual crop is active over its inclusive interval and adds its residue in its last month; cycles may overlap, each crop uses its own harvest index, and residues add up. [vault]
- RN-06 A perennial crop spreads its yearly residue over 12 months; partial years are not renormalized. [vault]
- RN-07 A month with no crop cycle is fallow, with zero plant input; soil cover is still given every month. [vault] (see `fechamento` P-13 on fallow months)
- RN-08 The screen shows each crop's yield unit (t dry matter/ha or t fresh product/ha). [vault]

### 5.3 Organic carbon

- RN-09 Organic carbon is entered in kg C/ha and modelled as farmyard manure, added after the month's decomposition. [RD-5] [vault]
- RN-10 Old compost entries given as physical mass are not converted to carbon without a known factor; what depends on them stays empty. [RP-3] [vault]

### 5.4 Crop catalog

- RN-11 Sugarcane keeps harvest index 0.85 (straw assumed removed, conservative for carbon credit) until GAIA decides its positioning on raw harvest (0.70). [vault]
- RN-12 Pasture (HI 0.77) means cut and hay; grazing is entered as explicit biomass input with improved grassland pools. [vault]
- RN-13 The grouped codes (crops, pulses, oilseeds) stay as a fallback; the screen offers the specific crops first. [vault]
- RN-14 A catalog change applies to new calculations only; past results keep their plant input. [RP-1]

### 5.5 Window and result

- RN-15 Each year of the window has 1 to 3 annual crop cycles, and none is left without one. [task BE-19]
- RN-16 The monthly data of both scenarios covers exactly the declared window, and every crop cycle, perennial yield, and organic carbon entry falls inside it; anything else is refused, naming the field. [task BE-22]
- RN-17 A calculation missing one scenario is refused as incomplete, never filled by copying the other one. [task BE-23] [RP-3]
- RN-18 The result can be cut to any month range inside the window; deltas, crops, and the organic carbon rate are computed again on that cut, never copied from the whole. [task BE-24]
- RN-19 The result lists the crops active in each scenario (soybean, maize), not the land use categories; a scenario entered as biomass lists none. [task BE-20]
