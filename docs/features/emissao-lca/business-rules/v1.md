# Emissão (LCA) · business rules v1

Date: 2026-11-03. Based on: vault notes routed by `/sync route docs/vault`, meeting 2026-09-01, the reference table decision of 2026-08-16, the meeting of 2026-08-14 and the tasks routed by `/sync route docs/tasks`, code in `gaia-api/lca/`.

Rules marked (P-n) are provisional: they follow the proposal until open point n is decided. The others are settled.

## 1. What the feature is

The technician fills, step by step, the crop, soil, inputs, fuel, and transport of one crop on one plot, and GAIA returns its emissions in CO2e (CO2 equivalent), per hectare and per kg of product.

## 2. Why

- The client needs the footprint of each crop to report it and to compare practices.

## 3. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Net | Emissions minus removals of a calculation |
| Annual soil management | The current soil practice, with a yearly removal or emission factor per hectare |

## 4. Journey

1. The technician opens the plot and starts an Emissão calculation.
2. They fill crop, soil (with land use change and current practice), inputs, fuel, and transport.
3. GAIA calculates and shows emissions, removals, and net.

## 5. Business rules

### 5.1 Form

- RN-01 Transport is the fifth step and is required; a missing transport is never read as zero. [vault, MVP contract] [RP-3] (P-1)
- RN-02 The crop field takes the shared crop catalog, the same codes Remoção uses. [vault]
- RN-03 Perennial, fruit, and forage crops (coffee, pasture, palm, mango, eucalyptus, avocado, citrus, cocoa) give zero crop residue emission on purpose, not an error. [vault]
- RN-04 A crop outside the catalog is refused with a clear error, never calculated as zero. [RP-3]

### 5.2 Land use change

- RN-05 Land use change is filled with selects only: the current use, one or more previous uses, and the years since each change; nobody types a carbon value. [vault]
- RN-06 Carbon stocks come from the reference table decided on 2026-08-16: the Município sheet of `LCA_Annual_Crops_Tool.xlsx`, checked cell by cell. [RD-3]
- RN-07 A change more than 20 years before the harvest year counts zero. [RD-2]
- RN-08 The stock sum is halved (total factor 0.5), as cell D41 of the spreadsheet does. [vault] (P-2)

### 5.3 Annual soil management

- RN-09 The soil step has a current management practice, picked from 11 practices, each with a factor in t CO2e per ha per year. [2026-09-01] [`Fatores de emissão Gaia.pptx`]
- RN-10 The yearly effect is factor × area: a positive result is a removal, a negative one an emission. [2026-09-01]
- RN-11 Net = emissions minus removals, and it can be negative (net sequestration); the dashboard shows removal as a green bar that subtracts from the total. [2026-09-01]
- RN-12 Land use change looks back 20 years and penalizes; annual management rewards the present. [2026-09-01]
- RN-13 Paulo Rocha's table is the source of the practice factors; a new version of it replaces them for new calculations. [2026-09-01] [RP-2]

### 5.4 Product and inputs

- RN-14 The product is optional; the user only picks it from the platform's product table, and its allocation, yield, and process factors come from that table and show in the result, never typed. [2026-08-14, task BE-27] [RP-2]
- RN-15 The harvested amount is entered in tonnes and crop residue in t/ha. [2026-08-14, task BE-27]
- RN-16 Synthetic nitrogen, organic nitrogen, and urea come from the fertilizers picked, never typed; agricultural gypsum is recorded but emits nothing, since IPCC gives it no factor. [2026-08-14, task BE-30]
- RN-17 The nitrogen share of a fertilizer comes from the catalog (sheet `Em_Processo_fertilizantes` of `LCA_Annual_Crops_Tool.xlsx`); its quantity is entered in kg/ha or t/ha. [task BE-31]
- RN-18 A pesticide is entered with its active ingredient concentration, in kg/ha, g/ha, ml/ha, or l/ha. [task BE-32]
- RN-19 Each input (fertilizer, pesticide, seed, fuel, electricity, transport) takes an evidence file in place of the old invoice field. [tasks BE-31 to BE-35]
- RN-20 Electricity uses one fixed factor per MWh (MCTI 2023), with no choice of grid mix. [task BE-34]
- RN-21 Transport emission is the distance in km × 0.1470 t CO2e per km, a business decision of 2026-08-14 confirmed by the product owner on 2026-08-16, replacing the Ecoinvent 3.9.1 factor (0.1047); biodiesel stays frozen for now. [task BE-35]
- RN-22 Climate comes from the farm's município (sheet `Clima` of `LCA_Annual_Crops_Tool.xlsx`: Tropical Seco is dry, every other class is humid); a climate the user sends is still accepted, and an unknown município asks the user. [2026-08-14, task BE-40]
